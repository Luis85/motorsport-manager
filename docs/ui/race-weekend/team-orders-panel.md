# Team and battles

## Integrated components

TeamOrdersPanel.

## Information contract

Two real driver summaries show intent and control ownership above cooperation, battles and shared-box tasks. Actual observed occupation/arrival and bounded pit priority remain separate from hoped-for outcomes.

## Interaction and safety

Both affected drivers are named for hold/yield/priority. Draft selection is not an instruction. Apply/cancel remains explicit and physical commitment takes precedence. Full battle details are readable in the decision drawer.

## Shared acceptance

Use the [layout](layout-system.md), [responsive](responsive-behaviour.md), [accessibility](accessibility.md) and [interaction](interaction-model.md) contracts. Rendering this screen does not create a command or alter pause/speed. Actual executable coverage is recorded in [verification](verification-completion.md).

## Finishing contract

`RaceTeamIntentTimeline` adds read-only accepted windows and already-issued bounded overrides. `RacePitServicePanel` adds actual approach/entry/queue/service/exit, the single whole-car timer and correlated completed visits. Open service is also available from the named driver’s More menu. No arbitrary future commands or per-wheel service times are introduced.
