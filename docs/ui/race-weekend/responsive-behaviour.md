# Responsive behaviour

## 1440×900+

Full timing tower, circuit and inspector/driver rail. Two driver cards remain persistent. Decision feed sits below the primary workspace.

## 1280×800

Reduce padding and inspector width before removing information. Deep inspector may hide timing while expanded.

## 1100×720 minimum

Core race decisions must remain reachable without horizontal scrolling. Context inspector may temporarily replace secondary timing space. HFlowContainer is required for action groups that may wrap. Text scale 115/130% is treated as a layout breakpoint, not only a font change.

The circuit remains the largest single region in Race view.
